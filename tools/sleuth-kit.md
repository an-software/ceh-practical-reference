# The Sleuth Kit

Category: Forensics. Purpose: Filesystem/disk-image forensic utilities.

## Basic workflow

1. Identify the image and partition layout.
2. Choose the filesystem-aware utility for metadata or recovery.
3. Preserve original bytes and record offsets.

## Troubleshooting

Wrong partition offsets or unsupported filesystems can produce misleading empty results.
