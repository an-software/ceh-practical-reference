# CrackStation

Category: Passwords. Purpose: Online hash lookup service linked in imported notes.

## Basic workflow

1. Use only an allowed synthetic hash.
2. Compare candidate algorithms with source context.
3. Verify returned plaintext by hashing it locally.

## Troubleshooting

Online submission may disclose credential material; no hit does not prove recovery impossible.
