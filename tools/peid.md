# PEiD

Category: Forensics. Purpose: Historical packer/compiler identification utility.

## Basic workflow

1. Identify the legacy build and supported format.
2. Inspect a copied sample.
3. Corroborate signatures with headers/disassembly.

## Troubleshooting

Old signatures may misidentify modern binaries; a packer match is not proof of malware.
