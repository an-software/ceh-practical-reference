# SecLists

Category: Passwords. Purpose: Reference collection of wordlists/payload lists, not an installed scanner.

## Basic workflow

1. Select the category relevant to the task.
2. Copy a minimal subset into the lab workflow.
3. Keep the full checkout in ignored external storage.

## Troubleshooting

Do not feed every list into every target; lists can be large and contain unrelated offensive data.
