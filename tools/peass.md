# PEASS-ng

Category: Privilege escalation. Purpose: Suite of local privilege-enumeration helpers.

## Basic workflow

1. Identify the platform and correct script/binary.
2. Review source and execution scope.
3. Corroborate highlighted permissions with native checks.

## Troubleshooting

Do not automatically execute downloaded tools or treat colored output as confirmed exploitation.
